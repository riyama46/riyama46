### Hi, I'm Rifaldi. I automate the busywork in HR.

Onboarding, approvals, reminders, HR inbox triage: the steps your people team repeats every week,
built as reliable workflows with **n8n** and LLMs like **Google Gemini**.

I've spent 4 years as a software engineer on the HR management system of a 13-company manufacturing group
in Indonesia (C#, ASP.NET, SQL Server, Azure), and I'm finishing a Master of Management with an HR focus.
I know how HR data and HR processes actually work, not only the tools.

**What I automate**

- **Onboarding and offboarding**: form to welcome email, accounts, checklists, Day-1 meetings
- **Requests and approvals**: leave, overtime, reimbursements, routed to the right manager
- **HR inbox and FAQs**: LLM-drafted replies and ticket triage, with a human in the loop
- **Documents**: offer letters, contracts and certificates generated from your templates
- **Reminders and reports**: probation ends, contract renewals, weekly headcount summaries

**Featured projects**

- **[HR Onboarding Automation](https://github.com/riyama46/hr-onboarding-automation)**: one Google Form submission triggers a Gemini-written
